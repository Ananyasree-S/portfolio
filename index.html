02 / Voice AI · Tool Calling

SupportFlow AI — Agentic Voice SDR

A real-time voice sales agent that runs natural, multi-turn discovery calls and autonomously decides when a lead is qualified.

  • End-to-end pipeline with Faster-Whisper STT, Ollama/Gemma reasoning, and streaming TTS for natural, low-latency speech.
